What this alpha does

Rebirth Planner: choose your Super Rebirth cycle and Rebirth level, then see the three required droids, minimum variants, Credit requirement, and when each required droid is safe to sell in the selected cycle.

Fusion Recipes: browse all 17 currently known community Fusion recipes, grouped by resulting rarity. Fusion recipes are marked Pending until verified in game or by a developer source.

Alpha 0.5: adds the DroidAtlas Industrial Command visual identity. Rebirth uses the orange command module; Fusion uses the blue command module. Functionality and data are unchanged from Alpha 0.4.

Sell badge: the number is the last Rebirth where that droid is required. The badge material/color shows the minimum variant required at that final Rebirth. Sell it after completing the number shown.

Minimum variant means that variant or any higher variant. Stellar is currently the highest standard variant.

Source status: exact requirement rows are currently seeded from the community tracker and remain Pending until promoted. Credit values through RB27 are confirmed unchanged in game.

This alpha intentionally leaves Fusion, production optimization, SC spawn rates, and other side systems out unless they become necessary to answer the core Rebirth question.
